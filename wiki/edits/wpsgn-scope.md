To: info@wpsgn.ca
Subject: Licence for the West Parry Sound civic address layer — use in OpenStreetMap

Hello,

I'm a volunteer contributor to OpenStreetMap (https://www.openstreetmap.org), the free, openly
licensed map of the world. I'd like to use the West Parry Sound Geography Network's civic address
points to check and fill gaps in OpenStreetMap's addresses for the district. Before any of that
happens, I want to be sure the data is licensed for it. I have three questions.

**1. Which licence covers the layer?** The only licence I can find for WPSGN data is the *Open
Government License – Township of The Archipelago* (version 2), at
https://wpsgn.ca/OpenGovernmentLicense%E2%80%93TownshipofTheArchipelago.pdf. It is linked from the
network's re3data entry (r3d100013016), but not from the address layer itself
(https://www.wpsgn.ca/s22/rest/services/wpsgnPublic/wpsgn_opendata/FeatureServer/0, ArcGIS item
c9053cbee22e47ceac167971a9963ed7), whose licence and access fields are empty.

That licence names the Township of The Archipelago as the Information Provider. The address layer,
however, covers thirteen communities: Seguin, Parry Sound, McDougall, Carling, McKellar, Whitestone,
Wasauksing First Nation and others. Could you confirm whether that licence covers the whole layer,
including every member municipality's addresses? If it doesn't, which licence applies to the other
members' data, and who could confirm it?

**2. Could the licence be attached to the data?** If the licence does cover the layer, it would help
everyone who uses it to have the licence named in the ArcGIS item's "Terms of Use" (licence) field,
or in the service's copyright text. Then anyone can see the terms where they download.

**3. A small fix in the licence document.** The "Records" definition still contains a drafting note
from the template: *"(this definition is not necessary if "Records" is omitted from clause 6B)"*. It
has no legal effect, but you may want to remove it in a future version.

If it's simpler, the OpenStreetMap Foundation also has a short permission form that a data provider
can sign to allow use in OpenStreetMap directly:
https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Waiver_and_Permission_Templates. Either route works for us.

Attribution would read: "Contains information licensed under the Open Government License – Township
of The Archipelago", or whatever wording you prefer. It would be recorded on
https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Contributors alongside other Ontario municipalities, such as Guelph,
Kingston and Oakville.

One unrelated thing you may want to know: on 11 October the address service answered "Service
wpsgnPublic/wpsgn_opendata/MapServer not started".

Thank you for publishing this data, and for any help.

Kind regards,
skfd
