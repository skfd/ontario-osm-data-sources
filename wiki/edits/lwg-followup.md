**Added to Contributors under your self-service procedure.** Following "Evaluating Canadian
Open Data Licenses" on https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/OGL_Canada_and_local_variants, I diffed
each licence against the Ottawa Open Data Licence 2.0. Every difference in the six below is
either one of the four listed kinds (locality, venue, PII definition, records definition) or a
clause that already appears in a licence on your list, which I name. I have added them to
https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Contributors#Ontario. The full diffs, with both sides
quoted, are at https://github.com/skfd/ontario-osm-data-sources/tree/main/licences.

- **County of Dufferin** — Open Government Licence – The County of Dufferin. Only locality,
  Ontario courts, PII via FIPPA s.2(1), and a FIPPA "Records" definition. Its exemptions match
  Ottawa's four exactly.
- **City of Barrie** — Open Government Licence – Barrie. Adds one exemption for information
  or records not accessible under MFIPPA, the same clause as Oakville, Niagara Region and
  Toronto.
- **City of Windsor** — Open Government Licence – The Corporation of the City of Windsor.
  Adds the same MFIPPA exemption, and says "official marks" for the crest/logo exemption, as
  Niagara Region does.
- **Haldimand County** — Open Government Licence – Haldimand County. Toronto's approved
  licence word for word, apart from the locality and the statute cited for "Records".
- **County of Huron** — Open Government Licence – The County of Huron. Adds an MFIPPA/PHIPA
  exemption (as Toronto and Hamilton) and a PHIPA definition that the operative text never
  uses.
- **City of Greater Sudbury** — Open Data Licence – City of Greater Sudbury. Its differences
  are Quinte West's: the exemption for anything not accessible under MFIPPA, PHIPA "or any
  other act or by-law", personal health information within personal information, city
  by-laws in the governing-law clause, and "Use includes downloading".

If you see any of these differently, tell me and I will take the entry down.

**A correction.** If my earlier message described the City of Brantford licence as a clone,
that was wrong. It is a rewritten licence: the City may cancel or suspend it "without notice
and for any reason whatsoever", users waive "any and all claims" against the City and its
officials, and it adds entire-agreement and non-waiver clauses. Hamilton's approved licence has
a similar cancellation clause, but Brantford goes further, so it needs your review.

**For your review** (not added to Contributors):

- **City of Sarnia** and **County of Lambton** — both add the sentence "use … shall be at your
  sole and exclusive rights" (probably meant "risk") and drop the Versioning clause.
- **County of Frontenac** — adds "do not rely on it for navigation, decision making or
  advising".
- **United Counties of Leeds and Grenville** — Ottawa 2.0 clause for clause, but with typos
  inside definitions (Personal Information defined as "perfect information").
- **County of Lennox and Addington** — the grant reads "licence to use the information for
  commercial purposes" (without "including"), and "Information" is redefined.
- **Town of Milton** — replaces the third-party-rights exemption with one for information not
  disclosable under MFIPPA s.10(1).
- **City of Brantford** — as above.
- **Regional Municipality of Durham** — Region of Durham Open Data Licence v1.0, which is not
  OGL-based (optional attribution, credit removable on request, use must be "not harmful to
  others").
- **West Parry Sound Geography Network** — Niagara Region's text with the Township of The
  Archipelago's name and leftover template text in the Records definition. The licence names
  one township on a layer covering thirteen communities; I am asking the network to confirm
  its scope separately.
